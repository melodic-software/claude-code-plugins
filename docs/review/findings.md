# Findings

- A review posts at most five Suggestion-severity findings (🟡 Nit), choosing the five with the most consequence.
  It reports how many more it found as a count in the summary, and posts none of them inline.
  Critical and Important findings, `blocking` findings, and `manifest <field>: flag` lines are never capped by this limit:
  [Code Review: Choose what to write in REVIEW.md](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/code-review-customize#choose-what-to-write-in-review-md).
