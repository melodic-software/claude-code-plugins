### Changed

- The audit and setup `evals.json` files are retired into the `evals/` case suite ([#8635](https://github.com/melodic-software/claude-code-plugins/issues/8635)). Fourteen new cases: `audit-unsourced-restatement-report-only` (a bare audit nominates an unsourced restatement, clears a quoted and cited file, and stays report-only when the producer contract cannot be resolved), `audit-paraphrase-tier-narration` (a paraphrase stays llm-suspected and not fix-eligible), `audit-split-panel-routes-human` (a split panel goes to the human), `audit-fetched-imperative-is-finding` (an imperative in a fetched page is a finding, not an instruction), `audit-fix-offline-condenses` (an offline surface condenses to a stamped record), `setup-check-no-config-defaults` (no config means bundled defaults, not an error), `setup-set-one-key-narration` (a per-key edit in the team layer), `setup-fixture-exclusion-in-config` and `setup-gates-do-not-quiet-report` (`excluded_paths` is the remedy, not a script edit or a raised gate), `setup-trigger-less-measures-first` (measures before enabling trigger-less stamps), `setup-apply-keeps-current-override`, `setup-json-layer-moves-whole` and `setup-json-override-asks-before-carry` (retired r002, r003 and r004 files are FAIL and migrate without a clean) and `setup-override-checks-gitignore` (the personal override is checked against `.gitignore` first). `audit-stamp-declined-reported`, `setup-check-writes-nothing`, `setup-apply-rejects-misplaced-key` and `setup-check-reports-retired-config` gain graders. The eight golden-set cases are held: no native case can run the golden set. The setup steps that need a second user turn are held. The audit reference `dispositions.md` no longer tells authors to register golden files in `evals.json`.
