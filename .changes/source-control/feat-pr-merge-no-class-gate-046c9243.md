### Changed

- The unattended merge gate no longer gates on work class. Eligibility reads no `work-class:` label except the trust hold: a pull request whose linked issue carries `work-class: untrusted-provenance`, or had it removed by a non-human, is still refused (`untrusted-provenance`, `untrusted-provenance-removed-by-non-human`), and unreadable issue labels refuse as `issue-labels-unreadable`. The `class-missing`, `class-unknown`, `class-provenance:*`, `class-label-removed-by-non-human` and `class-above-rung` refusals are gone.
- Eligibility now needs the linked issue vouched for by a human, refusing as `issue-not-vouched:<why>` otherwise. Either a write-access human wrote the issue, or one applied the tracker's admission label in person with no body edit after it; either suffices. The admission label is the `autonomous-eligible` role in `.work-item-tracker.json` at the base branch, `agent-ready` by default. A human author's issue also refuses when a non-human edited its body since (`body-edited-by-non-human`, read from the same edit history as the demotion record) or renamed it, or anyone did through an App (`title-edited-by-non-human`, read from the timeline's `renamed` events, since a title edit leaves `lastEditedAt` empty), and an admission label refuses when the title changed after it (`title-edited-after-label`), or refuses as `issue-not-vouched:no-labeled-event` when the timeline holds no `labeled` event for it. This takes the place of the class-label provenance check as the gate's human vouch. On the local path, an issue the lanes App both filed and admitted now refuses as `issue-not-vouched:labeler-not-human` until a person re-adds the label. It fails closed, and the pr-merge lane is unaffected.
- Promotion evidence still gates the merge, keyed by the effective rung instead of a work class: the C2 rung needs `C2-auto-merge` promoted, and the C3 rung also needs `C3-auto-merge` and `C3-ai-review-blocking`. The refusal reads `promotion-unqualified:<rung>` where it read the class. The resolver call, its lane-writable checks, `unattended.promoted` and the demotion window restart (`--window-start`) are unchanged, so after a demotion no merge happens at the rung until its cells are re-ratified.
- The risk score reads no work class: the `class-missing`, `class-above-rung`, `class-contradicted` and `blast-radius-missing` reasons are gone, and `RiskInput` drops `work_class`, `rung`, `blast_radius` and `c2_contradictions`. The gate no longer reads `merge.diff-check.c2-contradictions`. The key is deprecated with no effect, and the schema still accepts it; set to anything but false, it adds a note to the config notes, which the `unattended` report now carries as `configNotes`.
