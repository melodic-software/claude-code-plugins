### Added

- Shared interview: once its round has closed, a locked decision accepts a late Disagree. Votes, Passes and answers are still refused. One late Disagree on a locked decide question marks it "possibly reopened" in the owner's Inbox, with Keep decision and Unlock, and in every digest until the owner keeps, changes or unlocks it. Every question that depends on it shows "Needs review: <Q> may be reopened" to everyone. The decision itself never changes on its own.
- Shared interview: the owner reads a decision history, folded under each question and session-wide under Decisions. It covers locks, changes to the locked text, unlocks, closed and reopened polls, late Disagrees and kept decisions, each with its time. Sessions saved before this release start with an empty history.
- Interview shared lane: when to open a new round, what `dependsOn` names, and what to do with a possibly reopened decision in the digest.
