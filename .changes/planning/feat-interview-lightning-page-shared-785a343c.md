### Added

- `/planning:interview` lightning style on the page and in shared sessions: a new `set-style` op (`style`: `standard` or `lightning`) stores the session's style, and under `lightning` each question shows as a short card (a `(major)` tag on a question marked `major`, the recommendation in bold, its basis after a dash, the other options' letters and short labels, and any Flips if, Second view and Departs from line) that expands to the full question. In a shared session only the owner changes the style, from Settings ("Lightning cards"), guests see the short cards, and a lightning round holds up to 8 questions whatever the round cap. The answer choices, each commitment's confirm row and the restatement stay whole.
