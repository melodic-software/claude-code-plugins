### Added

- `/planning:plan` and `/planning:interview` name, before presenting a plan step or an option that adds a mechanism (a branch, workflow, ruleset, app, service, file, dependency or check), the existing control that already covers the same risk, and drop the mechanism when one does. An abstraction for a stated, known repetition with a namable shape is not dropped; the standards index's `simpler-code` and `reference-dont-duplicate` entries draw that line. A non-trivial new mechanism goes to `/overengineering:justify` on the proposal. Two interview eval cases cover each side.
