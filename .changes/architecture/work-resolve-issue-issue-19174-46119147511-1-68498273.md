### Added

- The map views and the deepening report take their palette and type stacks from the shared token layer (`reference/html-tokens.css`) and apply a consumer's `branding` from the rendered-views team file and personal override, or from the new `--branding-option` flag of `scripts/build-view.mjs`. A logo and its label sit in the first slot of the page's chrome bar. An override below the contrast floor is dropped with a notice on stderr.

### Changed

- The map view drops its own blue palette and scheme flip for the shipped token look, and gains the chrome's system, light and dark toggle. With no `branding` set, the deepening report's styles are unchanged.
