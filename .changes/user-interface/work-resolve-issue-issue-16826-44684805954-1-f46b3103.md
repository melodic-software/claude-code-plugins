### Changed

- Five pointer records lose the recheck trigger left behind when their as-of dates were removed: the reflow pointer in `reference/types/touch-web.md`, the `line-clamp` pointers in `reference/types/web/typography.md` and `typography/line-behavior.md`, and the WCAG pointers in the glossary's `color.md` and `interaction.md`. A pointer stores no value, so it has nothing to recheck.
