### Added

- **`/overengineering:audit` honors the `rendered-views` `medium` preference.** A `--medium terminal` argument, or `medium: terminal` in a `rendered-views` layer, drops the offer to render the findings through `/visualization:visualize`. `file` keeps the offer and passes `file` to that skill, so the view stays local. Every other value keeps the offer, and the terminal summary names the source of `medium`.
