### Added

- A settings card for the bedroom theme: `/bedroom` alone, in the desktop app, shows a card in the conversation with a button for each switch showing its state, steps for dim, glass and blur, a text field for each title, and reset, calibrate and the theme on or off. Each button runs the same `/bedroom` command a person would type, and the card follows changes made by clicks in the room. Away from the desktop app, `/bedroom` still answers in text alone.
- `/bedroom status`: what is on and off, the answer `/bedroom` alone gives.
