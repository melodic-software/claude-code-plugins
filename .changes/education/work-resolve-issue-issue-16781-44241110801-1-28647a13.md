### Changed

- `teach` now lists the directory a topic's slug would sit in before it gives or creates a workspace path, because different raw names can share one slug ([#16781](https://github.com/melodic-software/claude-code-plugins/issues/16781)).

### Removed

- `teach` no longer names a language or a tool in three spots: the list of manifest files in codebase mode's structure survey, the `.cursor/rules/` entry among the guidance files it reads, and the `C++`/`C#` example of a slug collision in `SKILL.md` and `context/lessons.md` ([#16781](https://github.com/melodic-software/claude-code-plugins/issues/16781)).
