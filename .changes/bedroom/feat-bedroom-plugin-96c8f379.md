### Added

- The `bedroom` plugin, off by default: a mod for the Claude Code desktop app that sets a dark theme whose backdrop is a teenager's bedroom at dusk, drawn as one SVG with a lighting shader at 30 frames a second. A click on the television, VCR, console, computer, ceiling light, desk lamp or lava lamp switches it, answered by the shader without sending the room again.
- The `/bedroom` command: each switch on or off, `dim`, `glass`, `blur`, `title console <text>` and `title tape <text>` for your own titles (kept on your machine; the defaults are made up), `instant`, `stats`, `calibrate`, `reset`, `on` and `off`.
