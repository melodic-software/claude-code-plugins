### Removed

- The instruction-count recipe no longer picks a tool for you: it takes its simulator, and which event is the instruction count, from the repository's own conventions or `/discovery:research`, and reports the recipe unresolved and skips it when neither settles one. The two tool verification records behind it are gone, as are the Cachegrind manual pointers in `/performance:target` and the README.
- The layout-shift and speculative-loading pointers in the techniques catalog now point at the source post's sections only, not at one platform's documentation.

### Changed

- The examples in `/performance:target`, `/performance:goal` and `/performance:snapshot` that named Python now describe a wrapper, a program or an interpreter path in stack-neutral terms.
