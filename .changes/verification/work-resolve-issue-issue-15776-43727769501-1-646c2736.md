### Changed

- `/verification:confirm` now gives the exact form of the `Observation check:` entry in Stage 2,
  one per failed check: the suspected observation, what makes it suspect, the second route with
  its result, and whether that ruled the observation in or out. It goes above the verdict, as an
  Assessment line in the outcome and refactor reports and as the Evidence row in the fix report.
- Eval case `confirm-failed-check-observation-first`: the `names-observation-check` grader also
  accepts "pre-dates", "prior to the rebuild", a time between "before the" and "rebuild" (for
  example "before the 14:09 rebuild"), and a time followed by "rebuild" within 40 characters (for
  example "file modified 14:02, rebuild finished 14:09").
