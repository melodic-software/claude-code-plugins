### Fixed

- `/verification:confirm` keeps the fresh-context verifier's verdict as returned. The producer never answers an open question in it or upgrades it, even when the verifier recommends accepting what it flagged. After a verdict below `CONFIRMED` it either sends what the verdict names back to implementation or dispatches a new fresh verifier whose brief adds at least one new fact quoted from a named source and nothing else, and the report and the evidence manifest list every verdict returned on that head beside the final one. Two new eval cases, `confirm-verifier-verdict-not-upgraded` and `confirm-every-verdict-listed`, cover it.
