### Changed

- `/verification:confirm` now says what happens when the fresh verifier's verdict comes back with an open question or a scope doubt ([#14045](https://github.com/melodic-software/claude-code-plugins/issues/14045)). The producing session records and publishes the verdict as returned. It never answers the open question and never upgrades the verdict, even when the verifier recommends accepting what it flagged. It either fixes what the verdict names or dispatches a new fresh verifier. That verifier's brief adds only facts: a scope rule quoted from its source, no wanted verdict, and none of the earlier verifiers' recommendations. The report and the evidence manifest list every verdict returned on a head beside the final one.
- New eval case `confirm-verifier-verdict-not-upgraded`: a verifier returns NEEDS WORK and recommends accepting a test edit. The run must record no CONFIRMED for that head and must name the fix or re-dispatch path.
