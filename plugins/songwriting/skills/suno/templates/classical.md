# Classical / cinematic template

Orchestral cinematic by default. For chamber or solo piano variants, swap descriptors.

## Style prompt

```
cinematic orchestral, sweeping strings, brass fanfare,
timpani, epic and heroic, no vocals,
90 BPM, D minor adagio building to maestoso,
hi-fi orchestral hall reverb
```

## Lyrics shell

Most classical / cinematic prompts are **instrumental** — leave the lyrics field with `[Instrumental]` or `[Melodic Instrumental]`. If you do want vocals (operatic, choral), use the structure below.

```
[Intro]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Sparse, single instrument — solo violin or piano
Set the emotional theme

[Verse 1]
[Operatic]
Latin / Italian / extended-vowel English works best
Long phrases, melismatic delivery
Single emotional thread per verse

[Chorus]
[Choir]
Soaring, layered SATB voices
Title phrase or thematic phrase
Repeat with orchestral swell

[Instrumental Break]
Full orchestral statement of theme
[Crescendo]

[Verse 2]
[Operatic]
Develop from verse 1 — more vulnerable or more triumphant

[Chorus]
[Choir]

[Bridge]
[Decrescendo]
Strip to solo voice + cello
Quietest moment of the piece

[Crescendo]
[Chorus]
[Outro]
[End]
```

## Tweak knobs

- **Subgenre swap:** `cinematic orchestral` → `Romantic-era symphony`, `Baroque chamber`, `solo piano`, `string quartet`, `minimalist contemporary`, `film score`
- **Mood:** `epic and heroic` → `melancholic and longing`, `dark and ominous`, `serene and pastoral`, `chaotic and fearful`
- **Tempo:** 90 BPM adagio → 60 BPM largo (slow) → 120 BPM allegro → 144 BPM presto
- **Key:** D minor (default dark) → C major (heroic) → A minor (somber) → F major (pastoral)
- **Build pattern:** `building to maestoso` (slow climb) → `staying restrained throughout` → `unexpected fortissimo bursts`

## Common variants

**Solo piano:** `contemporary solo piano, intimate room mic, melancholic and contemplative, 65 BPM, A minor, no other instruments, soft pedal warmth`

**String quartet:** `string quartet, classical chamber music, two violins viola cello, 100 BPM, G major, dynamic interplay, no other instruments`

**Film score (action):** `cinematic action score, propulsive ostinato strings, brass hits, taiko drums, 130 BPM, D minor, dramatic and urgent, no vocals`

**Choral / sacred:** `sacred choral music, SATB choir, pipe organ, Latin text, 80 BPM, C major, cathedral reverb, hi-fi production`

**Minimalist:** `minimalist contemporary classical, looped piano motif, slow harmonic evolution, 70 BPM, A minor, intimate and meditative, no orchestration`
