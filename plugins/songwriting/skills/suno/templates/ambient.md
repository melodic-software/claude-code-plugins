# Ambient template

Dark ambient default. For brighter / more melodic variants (Brian Eno-style, ambient pop), swap descriptors.

## Style prompt

```
dark ambient, ethereal pads, distant echoes,
field recordings underneath, slow harmonic evolution,
60 BPM, no percussion, infinite reverb tail,
contemplative and otherworldly
```

## Lyrics shell

Ambient is **predominantly instrumental.** Leave the lyrics field with `[Instrumental]` or `[Melodic Instrumental]`. If you do want vocals (textural / wordless), use the structure below.

```
[Intro]
[Melodic Instrumental]
First two minutes — pure atmosphere
Single sustained note or drone

[Verse 1]
[Whispered] OR [Humming]
Sparse syllables — single words, fragments
Long pauses between phrases
Words act as another texture, not narrative

[Interlude]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Field recording layer surfaces — rain, wind, distant voices
Harmonic shift — one new chord

[Verse 2]
[Whispered]
Echo of verse 1 fragments
Slightly more harmonic content

[Outro]
[Decrescendo]
Slow fade across 60+ seconds
[Fade Out]
```

## Tweak knobs

- **Subgenre swap:** `dark ambient` → `bright ambient` (Brian Eno-style), `drone ambient`, `ambient techno`, `ambient pop`, `space ambient`, `dungeon synth`
- **Mood:** `contemplative and otherworldly` → `unsettling and dread-filled`, `peaceful and meditative`, `cosmic and vast`, `nostalgic and warm`
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM (default — barely felt) → no tempo at all (`tempo-free`, `rubato`) → 80 BPM ambient pop
- **Texture:** `ethereal pads` → `granular synthesis`, `tape-loop loops`, `analog modular drones`, `processed field recordings`
- **Reverb:** `infinite tail` → `cathedral reverb`, `spring reverb decay`, `dry intimate room`

## Common variants

**Brian Eno-style ambient:** `bright ambient, treated piano, soft synth pads, no percussion, slowly evolving, 80 BPM, warm hi-fi production, peaceful and contemplative`

**Drone ambient:** `drone ambient, sustained low frequency, harmonic overtones, no rhythm, 90-second slow evolution, monolithic and immersive`

**Ambient techno:** `ambient techno, soft 4/4 kick underneath, washy pads, dub delays, 120 BPM, hypnotic and minimal`

**Space ambient:** `space ambient, vast synth pads, tape echo, distant radio static, 50 BPM, cosmic and isolated, infinite reverb tail`

**Dungeon synth:** `dungeon synth, lo-fi medieval keyboard textures, ethereal female humming, 70 BPM, cassette tape warmth, melancholic and mythic`
