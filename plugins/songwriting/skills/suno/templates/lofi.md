# Lo-fi template

Lo-fi hip-hop / chillhop instrumental. The "study beats" canonical sound. For lo-fi indie or bedroom-pop variants, see `pop.md` "Bedroom pop" common variant.

## Style prompt

```
lo-fi chillhop, relaxing study beats,
warm Rhodes piano, soft brushed drums, vinyl crackle,
72 BPM, no vocals, tape hiss saturation,
mellow and nostalgic
```

## Lyrics shell

Lo-fi is **typically instrumental.** Use `[Instrumental]` or `[Melodic Instrumental]` in the lyrics field. If you want vocal samples (chopped), use the structure below.

```
[Intro]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Vinyl pop, dust, then drums fade in

[Verse 1]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Main loop — Rhodes + drums + bass
8-16 bars, repetitive, hypnotic

[Bridge]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Add saxophone or guitar lead over the loop
Slight harmonic variation

[Verse 2]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Drop the drums — just Rhodes + bass
Sparse, contemplative

[Bridge]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Drums return, lead instrument soloing

[Outro]
[Melodic Instrumental]
Fade with vinyl crackle continuing
[Fade Out]
```

## Tweak knobs

- **Subgenre swap:** `lo-fi chillhop` → `lo-fi jazz hop`, `lo-fi house`, `lo-fi indie`, `study beats`, `vaporwave`, `sleepy beats`
- **Lead instrument swap:** `Rhodes piano` → `electric guitar`, `saxophone`, `vibraphone`, `acoustic guitar`, `flute`, `piano`
- **Mood:** `mellow and nostalgic` → `melancholic and rainy`, `warm and sleepy`, `bittersweet`, `cozy and intimate`
- **Tempo:** 72 BPM (chillhop default) → 60 BPM (sleep beats) → 85 BPM (slightly more energetic)
- **Texture additions:** `rain sounds`, `coffee shop ambience`, `vinyl crackle`, `tape wow and flutter`, `cassette warble`

## Common variants

**Lo-fi house:** `lo-fi house, washed-out four-on-the-floor kick, tape-saturated synth chords, sub bass, 105 BPM, hazy and warm, no vocals`

**Vaporwave:** `vaporwave, slowed 80s pop sample, chopped and screwed vocals, lush pads, 60 BPM, VHS warmth, nostalgic and dreamy`

**Sleepy beats:** `sleep-aid lo-fi, soft ambient piano, brushed drums, deep sub bass, 60 BPM, rain sounds, no melody changes, peaceful`

**Lo-fi jazz hop:** `lo-fi jazz hop, sampled bebop horn line, head-nodding drums, walking upright bass, 78 BPM, vinyl crackle, smoky and warm`

**Study beats:** `lo-fi study beats, simple Rhodes piano loop, soft drums, no melodic surprises, 72 BPM, calm and focused, no vocals`
