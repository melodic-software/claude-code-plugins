# Folk template

Indie folk / acoustic singer-songwriter. For country-leaning variants, swap descriptors.

## Style prompt

```
indie folk, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, warm upright bass,
sparse brushed drums, intimate female vocals with slight rasp,
94 BPM, key of D minor,
no reverb wash, no synths, no drum machines
```

## Lyrics shell

```
[Intro]
[Verse 1]
Story-driven, first-person, concrete imagery
Specific places, names, objects — folk thrives on detail
Conversational tone, longer phrases OK here

[Chorus]
Title phrase — emotional anchor
Repeat with subtle melodic variation
Often a metaphor that recurs through the song

[Verse 2]
Develop the story — time passes, circumstance changes
Same melody, deepening emotional content

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
Strip to voice + single instrument
Reveal the emotional truth the verses circled
Slightly higher melodic register

[Chorus]
[Outro]
A final image, often unresolved
[End]
```

## Tweak knobs

- **Subgenre swap:** `indie folk` → `Americana`, `folk-rock`, `Celtic folk`, `appalachian folk`, `freak folk`, `chamber folk`
- **Lead instrument:** `fingerpicked acoustic guitar` → `clawhammer banjo`, `mandolin`, `dulcimer`, `nylon-string guitar`, `12-string acoustic`
- **Vocal:** `intimate female with slight rasp` → `warm male baritone`, `harmonized male-female duet`, `breathy whispery alto`
- **Tempo:** 94 BPM (mid) → 70 BPM (ballad) → 120 BPM (uptempo folk-rock)
- **Mood:** `melancholic and reflective` / `nostalgic and warm` / `dark and haunting` / `hopeful and steady`

## Common variants

**Americana:** `Americana, fingerpicked acoustic, pedal steel, brushed drums, weathered male vocals, 92 BPM, dusty road feel, no electronic instruments`

**Celtic folk:** `Celtic folk, fiddle lead, bodhrán drum, Uilleann pipes, female Gaelic vocals, 110 BPM, traditional and lilting, no modern production`

**Folk-rock:** `60s folk-rock revival, jangly 12-string Rickenbacker, harmonized vocals, tambourine, 120 BPM, warm analog mix, melancholic and political`

**Chamber folk:** `chamber folk, fingerpicked guitar, bowed cello, soft female vocals, 78 BPM, intimate room sound, melancholic and literary`

**Appalachian:** `Appalachian folk, clawhammer banjo, fiddle, harmonized male-female vocals, 100 BPM, mountain holler tradition, raw and honest`
