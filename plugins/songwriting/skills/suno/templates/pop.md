# Pop template

Mainstream radio-ready pop. Front-loads catchy hook, polished mix, female lead by default.

## Style prompt

```
synth-pop, 80s-inspired, euphoric and nostalgic,
shimmering analog synths, warm Moog bass, punchy drum machine,
ethereal female vocals with layered harmonies, 118 BPM,
polished radio-ready production, no autotune
```

## Tweak knobs

- **Mood swap:** `nostalgic and hopeful` → `melancholic and yearning` for ballad lean
- **Era swap:** `80s-inspired` → `2010s EDM-pop` or `90s teen pop` or `Y2K bubblegum`
- **Vocal swap:** `ethereal female vocals` → `breathy male vocals` or `androgynous vocals`
- **Tempo:** 118 BPM (uptempo) → 95 BPM (mid-tempo ballad) → 75 BPM (slow ballad)
- **Production:** `polished radio-ready` → `lo-fi bedroom-pop` for indie lean

## Common variants

**Bedroom pop:** `dream-pop, hazy and intimate, reverb-soaked guitar, lo-fi drum loop, breathy whispered vocals, 95 BPM, tape saturation, no autotune`

**Hyperpop:** `hyperpop, glitchy and chaotic, pitched-up vocals, distorted 808s, sugar-rush synths, 160 BPM, no breathing room, maximum compression`

**K-pop ballad:** `K-pop ballad, longing and tender, piano lead, lush strings, soaring female vocals with vibrato, 75 BPM, cinematic production, no rap section`
