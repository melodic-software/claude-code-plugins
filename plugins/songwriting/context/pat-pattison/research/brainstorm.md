# Brainstorm — From Nothing

The writer has nothing yet — no title, no idea, no fragment. Just intent
("I want to write something") or restlessness. This file routes the
"blank page" scenario.

Distinct from:

- `idea-to-title.md` — writer has an idea / seed / image but no title yet
- `fragment-development.md` — writer has a partial line / section
- `object-writing.md` — writer wants a daily sensory practice (no song target)

## When to load this file

Trigger phrases: "blank page", "no idea", "starting cold", "give me
anything", "I want to write but I don't know what", "where do I start",
"no inspiration", "help me get unstuck".

## Coach posture

Pre-commitment is the point. Do not ask the writer to commit to a title,
form, or POV. Pull material first. Commitment comes after material exists.

> Object writing is preparation, not a substitute for songwriting.

The brainstorm produces raw material. The writer mines it later — often a
day or two later — to find what's worth pursuing.

## Path A — Sense-bound entry (10 minutes)

The single highest-yield brainstorm path. Pat's Challenge 1 (*Songwriting Without Boundaries* (2011)) is the
template.

1. **Pick a seed category** — What / Who / When / Where.
   - **What** — a thing or event: mirror, arrow, broken cup, bus ticket
   - **Who** — a person seen or imagined: waitress, priest, exhausted drummer
   - **When** — a time: first snowfall, late evening, six in the morning
   - **Where** — a place: hotel bar, parking lot, old church, cliff by the ocean
2. **Pick ONE seed word** in that category. Don't deliberate. First word
   that arrives.
3. **Set a 10-minute timer.**
4. **Object-write** sense-bound (per `object-writing.md`): sight, hearing,
   smell, taste, touch, organic, kinesthetic. Follow sensory associations
   even when they leave the seed.
5. **Stop at the buzzer** — mid-word if that's where the buzzer lands.
6. **Mine** the page: underline strongest sensory images, surprising verbs,
   possible title seeds, metaphor seeds. Set aside what isn't useful.

The mined material becomes input for `idea-to-title.md` or
`fragment-development.md` in a later session.

## Path B — Lived-input entry (no timer)

If the writer resists timers, use the alternative input:

- What did you notice recently that's still with you?
- What scrap of language stuck in your head this week?
- What conversation, image, sound, scene won't let go?
- What feeling has been with you that you haven't named?
- What contradicts what you usually believe?

These are sourcing prompts. Pick ONE answer; treat it as a seed; route to
Path A's object-writing pass.

## Path C — Recent reading / listening / watching

If the writer is in a creative drought:

- What's the last lyric you envied? Why?
- What's the last image (photograph, painting, scene) that stopped you?
- What song are you listening to too much right now?
- What book / movie / article gave you a sentence you wanted to steal?

Mine the envy. The thing that stops you is pointing at material you have a
relationship with. Use it as seed input for Path A.

## Path D — Title cascade (when the writer wants TITLE-shaped material)

If the writer would rather generate title candidates than object-write
sense-bound:

1. Pick a category seed (a feeling, a place, a relationship type, a question).
2. Generate 10 title candidates rapid-fire. No editing.
3. For each: identify the stressed vowel, identify front-heavy vs back-heavy
   (per `phrasing.md`), identify implied POV.
4. Set aside the 2-3 that have the strongest sound + strongest implied
   emotional shape.
5. Route to `idea-to-title.md` for the next pass.

This is the Pat Title Game path (cross-ref `title-game.md`) without the
co-write context — solo title generation.

## What NOT to do in brainstorm mode

- Do not commit to a form. Form decisions belong after material exists.
- Do not commit to a POV. Same.
- Do not edit during the object write. Editing kills sense access.
- Do not stop the timer to "fix" something. The writer can fix after.
- Do not summarize the writing back as a "plan." Brainstorm is generation,
  not synthesis.
- Do not pre-judge the material. The mining pass (later) is the judgment;
  brainstorm is pure intake.

## Artifact pattern

For a brand-new song that has no slug yet, the brainstorm material lands in
`songwriting/songs/<slug>/ideation/seeds.md` (per the SKILL.md
"Artifact Persistence" layout) if a slug exists, or in
`songwriting/practice/<YYYY>/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` if the writer is doing pure
daily practice without a song target yet.

If no slug exists yet, the brainstorm output may simply live in the
conversation until a title locks; then create the slug and write
`ideation/seeds.md` with the mined material.

## Routing to next phase

After a brainstorm session:

| If material yields... | Route to... |
|---|---|
| A strong image / phrase / feeling | `idea` → `idea-to-title.md` |
| A partial line / section already | `fragment` → `fragment-development.md` |
| A title candidate | `title` → `hook.md` "Title generation" |
| Nothing usable yet | repeat Path A with a different seed; or shelve and try again tomorrow |
| Multiple usable seeds | shelve all but one; choose the one that's pulling hardest |

## When brainstorm IS the goal (no song target)

The writer may want pure daily practice (`daily-practice.md`) with no song
target. That's the 56-day curriculum, not a brainstorm session. Route to
`/songwriting:daily-practice` instead.

Brainstorm = intent to develop a song, but no material yet.
Daily practice = intent to train, no song target required.
