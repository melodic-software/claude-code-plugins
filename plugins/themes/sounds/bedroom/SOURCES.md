# Where the bedroom's sounds come from

Each loop and thunder roll is cut from a recording whose own page states CC0 or public domain, read on 2026-11-20.
Each was made mono at 22,050 Hz, high-passed, and set to a loudness: cicadas -34 LUFS, rain -30, downpour -25, storm
-21 (integrated), thunder at a true peak of -1 dBFS. A loop is 20 s, cut from the steadiest 21.5 s of the range used,
its last 1.5 s faded into its start at equal power. Two recordings of each kind ship so a listener can choose by ear
(`/bedroom sound pick <kind> 1|2`); the first is the default, CC0 before the Public Domain Mark.

`tv-on.wav`, `tv-off.wav` and `silence.wav` are made by code, with no recording. The storm's wind is made by code too.
