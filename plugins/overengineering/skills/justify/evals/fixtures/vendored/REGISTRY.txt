Vendored-copy registry
======================

Decision record 0009 sanctions this pattern. Each bundle carries its own byte-identical copy of the
shared helper so that a bundle installed on its own has no cross-bundle path to resolve.

Copies expected to stay byte-identical to the source of truth:

  source-of-truth   lib/util.txt
  copy              vendored/a/util.txt
  copy              vendored/b/util.txt

Maintaining check: verify-vendored-copies, which runs on every change and fails when any copy
differs from the source of truth. Last run: passing.

Adding a bundle means adding its copy to this list. Removing a copy without removing its bundle is
what the check exists to catch.
