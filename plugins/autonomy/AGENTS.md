# autonomy plugin: contributor conventions

Conventions for editing this plugin's markdown in the marketplace repository. They do not apply to
a session running the skill.

- **Coined hyphenated compounds trip the spell gate.** The CI spell check splits a coined
  hyphenated compound into parts and flags a fragment as wrongly spelled. Keep multi-word
  identifiers as backticked tokens (`run_link_prefix`) or plain words, and write "wrongly
  associated," not a coined hyphenated form, so the gate has nothing to split.
