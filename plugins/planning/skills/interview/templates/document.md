# Document template: the literal shape

Fill every `<placeholder>`; keep the section order. Order questions most-important-first; group under `##` theme headings once there are more than a handful. Each question keeps the `Q<N>` id of its register row, so a returned answer resolves that row.

```markdown
# <Document title>

**Purpose:** <why this document exists and the decision riding on it.>

**From:** <the user>. **To:** <the recipient>. **How your answers will be used:** <where they go>

## Context

<One paragraph orienting a recipient who wasn't in the user's head. Enough to answer well, not a page.>

## How to answer

<Deadline and rough effort.> Write your answer on the `>` line under each question: `yes` takes the recommendation, a letter takes that alternative, or write your own answer. Partial answers and "I don't know" are useful: flag anything you're unsure of rather than skipping it, and keep the question numbers.

## <Theme heading>

<One `##` section per theme. Under each, its questions, most-important-first. Every question is one idea, never compound, in the shared question shape, with the answer stub directly beneath.>

### Q<N>: <One-idea question?>

Context:
- <what drives the decision, one claim per line, each with its link or source, or labeled judgment>

My recommendation: **<answer>**. <Why it beats the alternatives, in one or two sentences.>
Basis: <the Context sources the recommendation rests on>

<Or, in place of the two lines above, for a question no evidence lets us recommend an answer to:>
Withheld: <the evidence that would settle it>

Alternatives:
- (a) <option>. <what it gains>, but <what it costs>
- (b) <option>. <what it gains>, but <what it costs>

>

## Anything else?

Anything we didn't ask that we should know?

>
```
