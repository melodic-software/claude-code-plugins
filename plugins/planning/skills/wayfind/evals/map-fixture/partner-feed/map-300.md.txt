# #300 Map: partner shipment feed

State: open. Labels: work-map. Assignee: none.

## Destination

A PLAN to replace the nightly partner CSV drop with a push-based shipment-status change feed for
our three logistics partners (Northwind Freight, Blue Heron, Kestrel Couriers), live before the
2027 Q3 peak season.

## Notes

- The current CSV export job is `jobs/partner_csv_export.py`; partners load it at 02:00 UTC.
- Partner complaint thread: status lag of up to 24 hours drives support tickets.

## Decisions-so-far

- Pick the change source (#301)
- Decide event granularity (#302)

## Not-yet-specified

Something about how partners recover after a long outage feels unresolved, but nobody can phrase
it yet: replay windows, backfill, or contract versioning might each be part of it.

## Out-of-scope

- Partner client SDKs or libraries: we publish a feed contract only and never ship client code
  (Should we ship partner SDKs? #305)
- Pricing and invoicing data: stays on the existing billing API
