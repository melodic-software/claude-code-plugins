# Interview checklist: invoice export

## Starting point

Customers ask for their invoices as files; today support exports them by hand.

## Settled

- When do exports run? Nightly at 02:00 UTC.

## Constraints

- Export files are CSV with a header row: the downstream ERP requires it.

## Self-answered facts

- The largest customer has about 4 million invoice lines a month.

## Glossary

- statement: one monthly export for one customer.

## Open

- Which delivery channel: SFTP, S3 or email? No recommendation yet.
- How long are generated files kept? No recommendation yet.

## Not yet phrasable

- Something about very large customers' exports feels unsettled.
