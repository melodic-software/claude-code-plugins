Score rendered to campfire.wav (mono 16-bit, 22050 Hz, gameboy preset).

    t75 o3 l2 @0 c g | o4 l4 @2 e g a g

The same score is plugins/retro-audio/examples/campfire.mml. This folder does not
import that plugin. Re-render there, then copy the WAV here:

    python3 <retro-audio>/scripts/mml.py --chip gameboy --rate 22050 \
      --score <retro-audio>/examples/campfire.mml \
      --out campfire.wav
