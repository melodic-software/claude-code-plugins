## Step 5: draw as you explain

When the explanation follows a chain from fetch to compile to package, sketch fetch to compile,
redraw and add package, then redraw and add the publish edge. Three small growing sketches beat
one crowded sketch, and the series is the opposite of a wall of boxes. Each sketch is small and
carries one idea. A single all-at-once sketch, especially one saved for the end, is a reference.
A single simple point needs no sketch at all; a picture earns its place by teaching, not by
decorating.
