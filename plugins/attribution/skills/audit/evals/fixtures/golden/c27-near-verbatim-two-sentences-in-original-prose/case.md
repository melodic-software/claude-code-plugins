# Lessons: how to draw while explaining

Each lesson in this course runs from a question to an answer the learner can repeat back.

- **Build the picture in stages.** When the point follows a chain from fetch to compile to
  package, sketch fetch to compile first, redraw with package added, then redraw once more with
  the publish edge. Three small growing sketches beat one crowded sketch, and the series is the
  opposite of a wall of boxes. Each sketch stays small and carries one idea. A single
  all-at-once sketch, particularly one held back for the end, is a reference. A single simple
  point needs no sketch at all; a picture earns its place by teaching, not by decorating.
- **Name the boxes the way the learner will.** Use their words for the stages, never ours.
- **End on the learner's own sketch.** Ask them to redraw the last stage from memory.

Lessons that skip the drawing step are marked in the course index.
