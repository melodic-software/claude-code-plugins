# Lorewiki: Signs of machine-written prose

Synthetic source page for the attribution golden set. Lorewiki is a fictional community wiki
invented for these fixtures. Nothing on this page describes a real product or reproduces text
from a real page.

Canonical location for the purposes of this case: `https://example.invalid/lorewiki/Signs_of_machine-written_prose`.

## Content

### Puffed-up importance

Words to watch: "a lasting monument to", "watershed moment", "cements its standing", "speaks to
wider", "storied heritage", "signals a turn", "shifting terrain", "lasting imprint", "firmly
anchored", "paving the way for".

Machine-written text often inflates the importance of its subject by tying it to some larger
story, even when no source makes that link.

### Notability by listing

Machine-written drafts of new articles often argue that the subject is notable by listing the kinds
of outlets that covered it, with phrases such as "covered by several outlets", rather than citing
what those outlets said.

### Shallow analysis

Text written this way often ends sentences with an "-ing" clause that claims significance without
support, such as "...underlining its place in..." or "...pointing to the weight of...".
